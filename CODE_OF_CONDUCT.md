# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise code, not
people.

Concretely, the things that get someone removed: harassment, sexualised
language or imagery, personal or political attacks, publishing anyone's private
information, and persistent disruption after being asked to stop.

The things that make this project work: taking a review comment as a question
rather than an accusation, saying "I was wrong" without ceremony, and writing
down why something is the way it is so the next person does not have to guess.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Halvern is not yet published and has no maintainer inbox. When it is, this
section will name one. Until then, raise anything with whoever gave you access
to this code.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
pull requests that are not aligned with this document, and will explain why when
they do.

Responses scale with the behaviour: a private word first, then a warning, then a
temporary ban, then a permanent one. Deliberate harassment skips to the end.
